Research guides

A simple overview of common research steps. Your school may use a different format, so follow your teacher's required structure.

1. Choose a focused topic

Select something relevant, manageable, and suitable for your grade level. Make sure information and sources are available.

2. Write the research problem

Explain what you want to understand. Turn the topic into clear research questions.

3. Review related literature

Find trustworthy books, articles, and studies. Take notes and record source details as you read.

4. Choose a method

Decide what information you need and how to collect it. Ask permission and protect participants' privacy.

5. Organize and analyze information

Present findings accurately. Do not invent, change, or hide data to make results look better.

6. Write the conclusion and references

Answer your research questions using your findings. Cite every source you used in the required format.

Chapter 5

Key findings, conclusions, and practical recommendations.
